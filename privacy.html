Privacy Policy

Application: Discharge Summary (dsum) · Website: https://dsum.shop/ · Effective Date: 18 November 2026

1. Overview & Local-First Design

Discharge Summary (dsum.shop) is an offline-first progressive web application built to help clinicians draft, format, print, and export A4 medical discharge summaries. By default (Local mode), your patient drafts, saved templates, defaults, and theme settings remain strictly inside your device's browser storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) and are never transmitted to any external server.

2. Google User Data Accessed

Cloud synchronization and colleague handover features use optional Google Sign-In via Supabase Auth. When you choose to sign in with Google, the application requests and accesses only basic profile identity information permitted by your Google account:

  • Email address — used to identify your doctor account, isolate your synced workspace, and verify recipients for patient summary handover.
  • Display name & profile picture URL — displayed in your signed-in dashboard header and colleague contact cards so doctors can confirm the identity of a handover recipient.
The application does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, Calendar, or any other sensitive Google workspace scopes.

3. How Data Is Stored & Processed

  • On-Device Storage: All discharge summary drafts, institution defaults, custom themes, and saved templates are cached locally in browser IndexedDB (ds-builder-v2) and localStorage so the app functions offline.
  • Optional Cloud Sync (Supabase): If you sign in with Google, your records, templates, frequently shared contacts, and preferences are synced over HTTPS to Supabase PostgreSQL under Row-Level Security (RLS) policies scoped to your authenticated user ID.

4. Doctor-to-Doctor Patient Details Sharing

When you explicitly share a patient summary from the app to a colleague's email address:

  • The app verifies that the recipient uses Google Sign-In before dispatching the handover payload.
  • Shared patient details are delivered only to the specified recipient via Supabase sharing tables / Realtime broadcast and local account scope, along with your sender name, sender email, and optional handover note.

5. Data Sharing, Third Parties & Google API Limited Use

Your data and Google user information are treated strictly as private operational data:

  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize user data or clinical records.
  • We do not serve advertisements or run third-party marketing/analytics trackers.
  • If you inspect Google Workspace domains during recipient verification, a standard DNS-over-HTTPS MX lookup (dns.google) is performed on the recipient's domain only — no patient details are ever included in that query.
  • Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Retention, Export & Deletion Controls

You remain in full control of your data at all times:

  • Export Anytime: Export individual patient drafts or templates as local JSON files or A4 PDFs directly from your browser.
  • Local Deletion: Delete individual records or clear browser site data at any time to wipe all cached information from your device.
  • Cloud Deletion & Revocation: Deleting a summary or contact removes it from your synced workspace. You may also revoke Google OAuth access anytime in your Google Account Permissions.

7. Contact & Open-Source Repository

For privacy questions or data deletion assistance, please contact the maintainer via the official repository: https://github.com/syed98hafiz/dsum.
